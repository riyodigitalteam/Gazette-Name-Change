What Is a Gazette Name Change?

A gazette name change is the legal process of recording your old and new name in an official government gazette. The gazette entry is public, permanent and recognised by every government department, so it becomes your primary proof that both names belong to the same person.

After publication, you use the gazette copy to update your name in:

  • Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID and driving licence
  • Passport and visa records
  • Bank accounts, demat, insurance and EPF
  • SSLC / PUC marksheets and university degrees
  • Property, tax and utility records

Example: Old name Anjali Reddy, new name Anjali Sharma after marriage. Once the gazette notification is published, every institution can match both names to one person without further questions.

Central Gazette vs Karnataka State Gazette: Which One Do You Need?

Most Karnataka residents should publish in the Central Gazette (Gazette of India). It is accepted nationwide, including by passport offices, central government departments and foreign embassies. State gazette publication is mainly used by Karnataka government employees, who follow a specific state procedure.

Who Can Apply for a Gazette Name Change in Karnataka?

Any Indian citizen living in Karnataka can apply, provided the reason is genuine and lawful.

  • Adults (18+) applying for themselves
  • Parents or legal guardians applying for a minor
  • Married or divorced individuals changing or restoring a surname
  • People with spelling errors or mismatched names across documents
  • NRIs with Karnataka roots, through Indian embassy attestation

You cannot use a name change to hide a criminal record, avoid loans or legal dues, or impersonate someone. Offensive names and names with unusual symbols are also rejected.

Step-by-Step Gazette Name Change Process in Karnataka

Step 1: Prepare and notarise the name change affidavit

The affidavit is your sworn declaration of the change. In Karnataka it is usually made on ₹20 non-judicial stamp paper (confirm the current value with your notary) and signed before a notary public.

It must include your old name, new name, father's or spouse's name, date of birth, full address, the reason for the change, and a statement that the change is not for any fraudulent purpose.

Sample affidavit wording (summary):

I, [OLD NAME], son/daughter/wife of [NAME], aged [AGE], residing at [FULL ADDRESS], Karnataka, do hereby solemnly affirm that I have changed my name from [OLD NAME] to [NEW NAME] for [REASON]. I shall hereafter be known as [NEW NAME] for all purposes. This change is not for any unlawful or fraudulent purpose.

Step 2: Publish the name change advertisement

The Department of Publication's guidelines ask for an advertisement in at least one leading local daily. In practice, most Karnataka applicants publish in two: one English and one Kannada newspaper. Some passport offices, banks and education boards ask for both.

Commonly used newspapers in Karnataka:

  • English: Deccan Herald, The Times of India (Bengaluru), The Hindu
  • Kannada: Prajavani, Vijaya Karnataka, Udayavani, Vijayavani

Pick an edition that circulates where you live. A Bengaluru resident should use a Bengaluru edition, not a Mangaluru one.

Sample advertisement format:

I, [OLD NAME], S/o / D/o / W/o [NAME], R/o [FULL ADDRESS], have changed my name to [NEW NAME] vide affidavit dated [DATE] sworn before Notary [NAME], [CITY]. Henceforth I shall be known as [NEW NAME].

Keep at least three original copies of the full newspaper page.

RIYO EXPERT INSIGHT: The most common rejection we see is a spelling gap between the affidavit and the printed ad. Read the proof copy letter by letter before it goes to print, and match it to the affidavit, not to memory. Classified name change ads in regional dailies are booked by the column-line, so a clean, short format also keeps cost down.

Step 3: Pay the gazette fee on Bharatkosh

Since October 2018, the gazette fee is paid online through the Non-Tax Receipt Portal at bharatkosh.gov.in. Choose the Department of Publication as the ministry/department and the name change purpose, then pay ₹1,100 for an adult. Download and print the receipt; it goes in your application file.

Step 4: Prepare and send the application to Delhi

Arrange the file in this order: cover letter, application proforma, affidavit, newspaper pages, ID proof, photos, Bharatkosh receipt and soft copy (if required). Send it by registered post or speed post to:

The Controller of Publications, Department of Publication, Civil Lines, Delhi-110054

Keep the postal receipt and tracking number.

Step 5: Track publication and download your gazette

After scrutiny, your notice is published in the Gazette of India, Part IV. Search for it on egazette.gov.in using your name or the publication week, and download the PDF. That PDF is your legal proof of the name change.

Special Cases of Gazette Name Change in Karnataka

Name change after marriage

You can adopt your spouse's surname, add it, or change your full name. Attach the marriage certificate from the Sub-Registrar and your spouse's ID proof. Some institutions accept a marriage certificate alone for a simple surname change, but a gazette notification removes all doubt, especially for passports and overseas records.

Name change after divorce

To restore your maiden name or adopt a new one, attach a certified copy of the divorce decree and state the reason in the affidavit.

Minor's name change

A parent or legal guardian signs the affidavit and application for the child. Attach the child's birth certificate and the parent's ID. The minor fee is higher than the adult fee, so confirm the current amount on Bharatkosh.

Karnataka State government employees

Karnataka government servants follow the Karnataka Government Servants (Procedure for Change of Names) Rules, 1967. You apply through your appointing authority, which forwards the notification to the Director of Printing, Stationery and Publications for publication in the Karnataka Gazette. Speak to your department's establishment section before you start.

Central government employees

Central government staff publishing in the Gazette of India usually need to route the application through their department, with an authorisation from the head of office. Check the employee-specific checklist from the Department of Publication.

NRIs from Karnataka

Swear the affidavit before the Indian Embassy or High Commission in your country of residence. Publish the newspaper ad in your Karnataka home city edition and apply to the Department of Publication as usual. A relative in India can handle posting the file.

What to Update After Your Gazette Notification (and in What Order)

Update your documents in this order. Each one makes the next easier because it becomes a fresh proof in your new name.

  1. Aadhaar: Through an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or the myAadhaar portal, using the gazette as proof of identity.
  2. PAN: Online correction request, attaching the gazette copy.
  3. Bank accounts and demat: Visit your branch with the gazette, new Aadhaar and new PAN.
  4. Passport: Apply for re-issue on Passport Seva; carry the gazette and newspaper pages.
  5. Voter ID and driving licence: Update through the Voters' Service Portal and Karnataka Transport (Sarige/Parivahan) services.
  6. Educational certificates: Apply to KSEAB (SSLC/PUC) or your university with the affidavit, newspaper pages and gazette.
  7. Employer, EPF, insurance, property and utilities: Submit the gazette copy to each.

FAQs: Gazette Name Change in Karnataka

How do I change my name legally in Karnataka? Swear a name change affidavit before a notary, publish the change in a local newspaper, and apply to the Department of Publication for a Gazette of India notification. Once published, the gazette is your legal proof.

What is the fee for gazette name change in Karnataka? The Central Government fee is ₹1,100 for adults, paid online on Bharatkosh. With affidavit and newspaper costs, a DIY name change usually totals ₹2,500 to ₹5,000.

How many days does gazette name change take? Publication usually takes 30 to 45 working days after the Department of Publication receives a complete file. End to end, plan for about 6 to 9 weeks.

Is one newspaper enough for name change in Karnataka? The Department of Publication asks for at least one leading local daily. Many applicants publish in one English and one Kannada newspaper because some passport offices, banks and boards ask for both.

Where is the gazette office for Karnataka residents? For the Gazette of India, applications go to the Department of Publication, Civil Lines, Delhi-110054. The Karnataka Gazette is handled by the state Director of Printing, Stationery and Publications, mainly for state government employees.

Can I apply for gazette name change online from Bengaluru? You pay the fee online on Bharatkosh and download the published notification from egazette.gov.in. The signed physical file is still typically sent by post, so check the Department of Publication's current instructions.

Do I need a lawyer for a gazette name change? No. A notary attests the affidavit, and you can complete the rest yourself. A lawyer or service provider helps only if your case is complex.

Is gazette mandatory to change name in passport? For a simple surname change after marriage, a marriage certificate may be enough. For other changes, passport offices generally ask for the gazette notification along with the newspaper ads.
